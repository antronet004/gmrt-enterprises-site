About GMRT Enterprises

A small, independent software studio.

GMRT Enterprises started the way most small software studios do: with one person building something they wished already existed, and deciding to finish it properly instead of letting it stay a half-done side project.

That first project became Calm & Clear, a wellness and study-habit app built around a simple idea — that most people don't need another app demanding their attention, they need a quiet place to check in with themselves for thirty seconds and move on with their day. From there, the studio grew to include Blockwell, a puzzle game built on the same underlying philosophy: respect the player's time, don't manipulate them into staying longer than they want to, and make the core loop genuinely satisfying on its own merits.

How We Build

We're a small team, and we keep our scope small on purpose. Every feature we ship has to earn its place — we'd rather leave something out than ship something half-considered. Before anything goes out to the public, it's tested by real people on real devices, not just simulators and screenshots.

We take data privacy seriously because our own apps are aimed at teens and adults navigating stress, focus, and mental load — a context where trust matters more than usual. Every app we publish has a real, specific privacy policy that says exactly what's collected and why, not a boilerplate template. We don't sell user data, and we don't build growth mechanics designed to keep people scrolling past the point of usefulness.

What "GMRT" Means to Us

GMRT Enterprises is the umbrella under which we publish everything we build. As the studio grows, every new app will carry the same baseline commitments: a real privacy policy, genuine testing before release, and a founder who reads support email personally.
