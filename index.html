Software, made with intent.

GMRT Enterprises is an independent studio building focused mobile apps for wellness, focus, and play — designed to respect the people who use them.

We build small, and we build things that matter to the people using them. Every product we ship starts from a simple question: what does this person actually need right now, and how do we get out of their way while they do it? That question shapes everything from our feature lists to our interface design to the data we choose not to collect.

We're not interested in engagement metrics for their own sake, dark patterns that trick people into subscriptions, or apps stuffed with features nobody asked for. We'd rather ship something small and honest than something large and hollow.
