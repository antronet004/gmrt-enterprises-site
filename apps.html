Our Apps

Two apps, one philosophy: build something focused, and respect the person using it.

Calm & Clear

A wellness and study-habit companion for iPhone and iPad, built to help you understand your mood, manage stress, and build steady habits — one small check-in at a time.

  • Mood and stress check-ins with trend tracking over time
  • A guided, animated breathing exercise to help you reset
  • A Pomodoro focus timer for structured, distraction-light work sessions
  • A CBT-inspired reframing exercise for working through a stressful thought
  • Goal setting with simple progress tracking
  • A short lesson library on mindfulness, stress, and habit-building
  • A dashboard showing your trends, streaks, and progress in one place
  • A local weather and air-quality snapshot tied to your check-ins

Blockwell

A clean, endless block-puzzle game for Android, with a daily challenge mode and cosmetic themes to unlock.

  • Endless mode with steadily increasing difficulty
  • A daily challenge with a fresh board each day
  • Unlockable cosmetic themes
  • Short, satisfying sessions — built to be picked up and put down easily
